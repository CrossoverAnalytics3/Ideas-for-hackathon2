What the page says

Verified means a known misconception produces exactly the digits written, and the code confirmed it. Unverified means no rule produces those digits, which usually means the reading auto-corrected the mistake, so we ask instead of guessing.

A ten-frame flashes for one second. Say how many. Then say how you saw it ("five and three"). Speed and accuracy are recorded; the patterns grow as you get them.
